# Photon Engine pricing and terms (Quantum 3 / Fusion 2 / PUN2) and dedicated-server hosting cost structure, as of 2026-10-08

Method note. Every page below was consulted on 2026-10-08. `www.photonengine.com` pages were read through the fetch tool; `doc.photonengine.com` sits behind a Gcore browser-validation interstitial that blocks the fetch tool, so those pages were downloaded with `curl` (browser User-Agent + Referer) and converted to text; the page's own "Last updated on" stamp is recorded for each. Hetzner's website renders prices through a web component that calls Hetzner's own price API (`https://website-price-api.hetzner.com/api/v1/products/{id}`, endpoint read from the site's JS bundle `/_resources/themes/static/dist/vendors.js`); the numbers below come from that API, not from a rendered screenshot. Tags: VERIFIED = read on the cited page/API; INFERRED = deduced from verified statements; UNKNOWN = not found. Photon's pages use long dashes in some sentences; in quotes below they are replaced by a colon or comma (house style), nothing else is altered. Prices are shown with the "$" sign only on Photon pages (no currency code, no tax/VAT note anywhere on the Photon pricing pages).

## KQ2. Pricing rules from the Photon pricing documentation (multiple apps per account, plan per app, CCU accounting, hard cap vs burst, behaviour at the cap, switching SDKs)

### Takeaway
Plans attach to an AppId (one active subscription per AppId), CCU is billed as the sum of monthly regional peaks across the whole application, the 20 and 100 CCU plans hard-reject extra clients (MaxCcuReached) while 500+ plans burst and bill overage, the free 100 CCU slot is one per studio/account (a Fusion or a Quantum app, not both) and frees up again when that app is upgraded; nothing in the docs describes switching an existing AppId to another SDK.

### Cited Findings
- CCU definition, VERIFIED: "CCU stands for Concurrently Connected Users: the number of users online at the same moment, counted across your entire application: all rooms and all regions combined. It is not a per-room limit." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- CCU billing, VERIFIED: "Photon bills on Peak CCU: the sum of the monthly peak CCU per region. Sample month: EU peak 420 CCU + US peak 230 CCU + other regions 0, CCU total = 650, matching a 1,000 CCU plan. The monthly peak counts even if usage drops afterward, so upgrade promptly after a spike to avoid overage charges." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Hard cap vs burst, VERIFIED: "Plans below 500 CCU (20 CCU and 100 CCU tiers) are hard-capped. When the limit is reached, additional clients are rejected by the server and disconnected: in client code this surfaces as a disconnect with cause MaxCcuReached." and "Plans from 500 CCU upward (including Premium Cloud) have CCU Burst: no player is ever cut off. The exceeding CCU is allowed immediately and billed as overage with the next invoice. After your first burst you have 48 hours to upgrade your subscription before your CCU may, at Photon's sole discretion, be capped at the agreed number." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Overage price per burst CCU, VERIFIED in the Quantum FAQ: "If you do not upgrade, we will send an 'overage invoice' ... and charge each CCU above your subscribed plan with a fee of $0.75 / $1.00 (based on the used SDK) per CCU." Which SDK gets which rate is not stated. [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/quantum/current/getting-started/faq) (Last updated on 23/06/2026)
- Offline sessions, VERIFIED: "Sessions that never open a connection to the Photon Cloud (for example Fusion GameMode.Single or PUN 2 Offline Mode) do not consume CCU." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Free-plan licensing, VERIFIED: "20 CCU free plan: no. It is licensed for development and non-commercial use only. Publishing a game on it, including releasing on Steam or app stores, is not covered by the license." and "100 CCU free plan: yes. It is licensed for launch and commercial use, limited to one application per customer and to Fusion/Quantum games only. A game monetized only through ads still counts as commercial use." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Rules of the free 100 CCU plan, VERIFIED (verbatim list): "One application per studio/account (a Fusion or a Quantum app, not both)." "Standalone: it cannot be combined with another subscription (e.g. a 500+ CCU paid plan), since only one subscription can be active per AppId. It can be combined with the one-time 100 CCU ... for a total of 200 CCU." "You cannot downgrade an app from a paid plan back to a free plan." "If you upgrade the app to a paid plan, the free 100 CCU slot becomes available again for another of your applications." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Stacking rule, VERIFIED: "Two subscriptions can never be combined with each other (e.g. free + 500 CCU is not possible), since only one active subscription per AppId is technical possible." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Activation of the free 100 CCU, VERIFIED on the launch blog post (2024-03-11): in the dashboard "select an application, click '-/+ CCU', select the 100 CCU tab then activate the Free 100 CCU plan"; "The Free 100 CCU plan is available for either a Photon Fusion or a Quantum app within your account"; "Apps currently on a 100 CCU paid plan will maintain their status and not automatically convert"; for additional apps "a paid plan is available at $95 for 12 months per app". [Source](https://blog.photonengine.com/new-free-100-ccu-for-photon-fusion-and-quantum-games/)
- Traffic overage billing, VERIFIED: "At the end of the month, the overage (total traffic minus included traffic) is invoiced automatically at the regional per-GB rate and charged to the payment method on file." Photon "sends a heads-up email in advance when projections show your app will exceed its allowance." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Fair-use guidance, VERIFIED: "Photon recommends staying below 500 messages per second per room: a fair-use guideline rather than a hard limit". [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Cloud tier switching, VERIFIED: "Switching between Public, Premium and Enterprise Cloud is seamless, with no service interruption." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- AppId creation is SDK-typed, VERIFIED: "On the dashboard select YOUR > APPS > Development and press CREATE A NEW APP. Select Fusion as Photon SDK. Select Fusion 2 as SDK Version." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/current/getting-started/appid-instructions) (Last updated on 21/07/2026)

### Inferences
- A PUN2 app and a new Quantum (or Fusion) app can coexist in one account: the docs speak of "one application per studio/account" for the free 100 CCU and "only one subscription per AppId", and the AppId creation flow selects the SDK per app. INFERRED: each AppId carries its own plan; the account is not the billing unit.
- The free 100 CCU slot is consumed by exactly one Fusion-or-Quantum app; it is not a pool shared by all apps, and it moves to another app only when the current holder is upgraded to a paid plan (verified rule), never by downgrading.
- Switching SDKs: no statement exists; since the SDK is chosen at AppId creation, the safe reading is that a new AppId is created per SDK (INFERRED).

### Gaps
- The docs never state outright "each application has its own plan"; this is inferred from the "per AppId" wording.
- No documented way to convert an AppId between SDKs (UNKNOWN; treat as "create a new app").
- The $0.75 vs $1.00 overage mapping to SDKs is not documented.

## KQ3. PUN2 pricing and whether a live PUN2 app consumes the Fusion/Quantum free 100 CCU

### Takeaway
PUN has its own, cheaper price list (free 20 CCU dev plan, $95 one-time 100 CCU, $95 / $185 / $370 per month, Premium $0.29 per CCU with a $580 minimum) and no free 100 CCU tier; the free 100 CCU is reserved for Fusion/Quantum apps, so an existing PUN2 app does not occupy it.

### Cited Findings
- PUN tiers, VERIFIED: "Development Only" Free 20 CCU, 60.0 GB per month, "Capped at 20 CCU - no CCU Burst"; "For Launch" 100 CCU $95 one-time for 12 months (0.3 TB, "For Games Only", "Capped at 100 CCU - no CCU Burst"); 500 CCU $95 per month (1.5 TB); 1,000 CCU $185 per month (3.0 TB); 2,000 CCU $370 per month (6.0 TB); same $0.05 / $0.10 per GB overage. [Source](https://www.photonengine.com/pun/pricing)
- PUN Premium, VERIFIED: $0.29 per CCU, minimum "$580" per month including 2,000 CCU and 6.0 TB; same 3.0 GB per peak CCU rule. [Source](https://www.photonengine.com/pun/pricing)
- PUN Plus asset wording, VERIFIED: "Same content as PUN FREE, plus a 100 concurrent user plan for the Photon Cloud (approx. 40k MAU, valid 12 months). Just $95". [Source](https://www.photonengine.com/pun/pricing)
- No free 100 CCU for PUN, VERIFIED by absence on the PUN page and by the doc rule "100 CCU free for development and commercial use: for Fusion/Quantum games only, one app per customer". [PUN](https://www.photonengine.com/pun/pricing); [Doc](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- The generic overview page prints the PUN/Realtime-style table ($95 / $185 / $370, Premium $0.29, $580 minimum), not the Fusion/Quantum one; use the product pages for Fusion/Quantum figures. [Source](https://www.photonengine.com/pricing)
- The Quantum FAQ repeats the exception: "One exception to this rule are the Free100 plans for Quantum and Fusion. These can be combined with 100 CCU coupons from the Unity Asset store (Quantum Plus and Fusion Plus)." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/quantum/current/getting-started/faq)

### Inferences
- INFERRED (strong): the PUN2 app sits on its own AppId with its own plan (20 CCU free dev, or whatever is booked) and has no claim on the Fusion/Quantum free 100 CCU slot; adding one Quantum or Fusion app and activating the free 100 CCU on it is allowed as long as no other Fusion/Quantum app in the account already holds the free slot.
- INFERRED: if the PUN2 app is shipped commercially on the 20 CCU dev plan, it is outside the licence ("licensed for development and non-commercial use only"); that is a separate compliance point, unrelated to the Quantum/Fusion plan.

### Gaps
- No page states "PUN plans are independent from Fusion/Quantum plans" in those words; the independence follows from the per-AppId rules.

## KQ4. Dedicated servers in Fusion Server mode: what Photon charges, how a server peer is counted, Photon-hosted options, and whether developer-hosted servers are allowed on the free 100 CCU plan

### Takeaway
Photon does not host or orchestrate Fusion game servers and charges nothing specific for them: a Public Cloud subscription (including the free tiers, by the docs' wording) is sufficient for Server mode, the developer pays a third party for the machine, and Photon's only metered items remain CCU and traffic; the claim that a server instance counts as one CCU exists only in now-unreachable forum replies, not in current documentation.

### Cited Findings
- Hosting responsibility, VERIFIED: "Fusion enables the development of games using the client-server topology. In this case, a dedicated game session instance runs on a dedicated server provided by a hosting provider which players connect to with their client." Required pieces: "the Fusion Headless build" and "the Dedicated Server Machine from your preferred Hosting Provider". [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/current/concepts-and-patterns/dedicated-server-overview) (Last updated on 21/09/2026)
- Photon subscription needed for Server mode, VERIFIED: "To run a game in Server Mode a dedicated game server is needed as well as a Photon Fusion Cloud subscription." and, under "Dedicated Photon Cloud Servers": "A subscription to the Photon PUBLIC CLOUD is sufficient! A Photon ENTERPRISE CLOUD subscription is only necessary to access the features & services which can only be offered in a dedicated environment." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/current/concepts-and-patterns/dedicated-server-overview)
- What the Photon Cloud still does in Server mode, VERIFIED: "The Photon Cloud handles: connection handling; relay fallback; and, state backup for server migration". Enterprise adds "the ability to run custom version of the Fusion Cloud Plugin (not available yet); Dedicated IPs; SLA; 24/7 NOC team; and more." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/current/concepts-and-patterns/dedicated-server-overview)
- No orchestration from Photon, VERIFIED: "Photon does NOT offer dedicated game session server orchestration. Orchestration services are usually offered by game session hosting providers themselves". Providers named: "Edgegap with the Asteroid sample", "Gameye", "PlayFab", "GameLift"; "It is of course possible to use custom solutions and standalone docker instances if so desired." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/current/concepts-and-patterns/dedicated-server-overview)
- Cost composition by mode, VERIFIED: "The cost of using Photon solutions is comprised of two factors: concurrent users (CCU) and bandwidth. CCU for all three Fusion solutions are the same. ... When using Server Mode, bandwidth pricing will depend more on the third party server provider." [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/current/fusion-choose) (Last updated on 28/07/2026)
- Pricing page framing, VERIFIED: "Hosting, operations and scaling services are all cared for by us. You build it, we run it." (this refers to the Photon Cloud relay/matchmaking servers, not to developer game servers; see hosting responsibility above). [Source](https://www.photonengine.com/fusion/pricing)
- Server peer counting as CCU: the only statements found are forum replies surfaced as search-engine snippets ("any and every peer connected to the Photon Cloud counts towards the CCU, so a Dedicated Server would not be different"; "all of them, except the Single Player mode ... count towards the CCU"). On 2026-10-08 `https://forum.photonengine.com/` and the thread URLs return a photonengine.com "404 - Page not found!" page, web.archive.org's index answered "Internet Archive: Temporarily Offline", and archive.ph had no snapshot; the quotes are therefore UNVERIFIED. Thread URLs as indexed: [thread 19531](https://forum.photonengine.com/discussion/19531/photon-fusion-architecture-modes-and-pricing); [comment 60662](https://forum.photonengine.com/discussion/comment/60662/); [comment 62974](https://forum.photonengine.com/discussion/comment/62974/)
- Offline-only exclusion in current docs, VERIFIED: only sessions that "never open a connection to the Photon Cloud" are excluded from CCU; a Fusion Server-mode peer does connect to the cloud (see "connection handling; relay fallback" above). [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)
- Photon Server (self-hosted) licensing, VERIFIED: "The Photon Server requires a valid license to run. Please contact us for any questions you may have!" and "Commercial Licenses: For commercial projects and higher CCU needs please contact us." No price and no free-licence tier printed on the page; licences are "Floating Licenses" that must reach Photon's licence monitor at start and at least once per 24 h. [Source](https://doc.photonengine.com/server/v5/operations/licenses) (Last updated on 14/10/2025); `https://www.photonengine.com/server` and `/server/pricing` both 301-redirect to [the Photon Server intro](https://doc.photonengine.com/server/v5/getting-started/photon-server-intro) (Last updated on 11/06/2026).
- Photon Enterprise Cloud as the only Photon-hosted "dedicated" option, VERIFIED: "Dedicated Photon Cloud Servers are offered by Photon. To request one, an Enterprise subscription is needed." Enterprise is "Minimum 2,000 CCU", price on request. [Fusion doc](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/current/concepts-and-patterns/dedicated-server-overview); [Pricing doc](https://doc.photonengine.com/photon/current/pricing)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: a developer-hosted Fusion dedicated server is allowed on the free 100 CCU plan (and on the 20 CCU dev plan), because the docs require only "a Photon Fusion Cloud subscription" and state that Public Cloud "is sufficient"; no page restricts Server mode to paid tiers.

- Photon Server self-hosted licence prices and whether a free low-CCU licence still exists: UNKNOWN (page says "contact us").
- Enterprise Cloud price: UNKNOWN (contact sales; minimum 2,000 CCU).

## KQ6. Third-party machine and orchestration prices for a headless Linux Unity server (Hetzner Cloud; Edgegap; Gameye; Hathora/GameFabric status)

### Takeaway
Hetzner publishes machine prices only (EUR net, excluding VAT, IPv4 add-on EUR 0.50 per month, large EU traffic allowances); Edgegap and Gameye publish per-vCPU orchestration prices (about $0.069 and $0.07 per vCPU-hour on demand); Hathora no longer exists as a game-hosting product (acquired by Fireworks AI, customers moved to Nitrado GameFabric, which publishes no numbers). All figures are prices of machines or vCPU time, not match capacity.

### Cited Findings
Hetzner Cloud (prices read from Hetzner's website price API on 2026-10-08; EUR values are net, "Country (VAT)" selector on the page defaults to "All others" with 0 % tax, EU VAT is added per country from the `/api/v1/tax-rates` endpoint; the page states "Price incl.
