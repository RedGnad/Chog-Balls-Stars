Every Chog Genesis is a hero. Which one is yours?

The traits of your Genesis decide how it fights. Type the number in its name, or the address of the wallet that holds it.

  • Outfit gives the class
  • Eyes give the attack
  • Aura gives the effect
  • Rarity gives the delivery

Genesis with the most wins

    The kit of each Genesis comes from its on-chain metadata and from rules that are the same for the 1,969 of them. The career belongs to the Genesis, not to the wallet: it follows the token when it changes hands. Results are reported by the players' own games and are not verified by replay yet.

    Open the game with the wallet that holds your Genesis to play it, or to lend it: a link lets a friend play your Genesis without a wallet, and their matches count for its career.
